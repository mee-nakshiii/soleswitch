### Project Description
[SoleSwitch is a contactless music controller that lets users control Spotify using
simple foot and leg movements captured through a camera.

Instead of reaching for your phone or keyboard, just move your feet to play,
pause, skip, go back and to play specific songs — because apparently our
feet needed another job.]

### The Problem 
[Sometimes your hands are busy, your phone is too far away, and reaching for
the music controls feels like an unreasonable amount of effort.

We decided this was a serious problem that absolutely needed solving.
]

### The Solution 
[SoleSwitch turns your feet into a music controller.

A camera tracks the user's feet using pose landmarks. The movement of the feet
is interpreted as gestures and converted into music-control commands.
]
